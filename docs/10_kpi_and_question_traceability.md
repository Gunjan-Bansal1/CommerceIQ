## 2. Explicit Data Feasibility Limitations

1. **`GEO-08` (Orders relative to population):** Cannot be answered directly from the 12 validated source datasets because state/municipal population denominators were not acquired. Documented rather than fabricated.
2. **Actual Profit, Commissions, CAC, ROAS, Refunds:** Out of scope per [`02_business_requirements.md`](file:///c:/Users/bgunj/Desktop/commerceIQ/docs/02_business_requirements.md); all monetary metrics report recorded BRL merchandise/freight/payment values, and Phase 14 decision simulations separate observed baselines from explicitly labeled illustrative margin/cost parameters.
3. **Marketing Seller Activation (`MKT-08` / `KPI-MKT-05`):** 462 of 842 closed deals have no matching `seller_id` in `sellers`; missing matches are reported as incomplete cross-dataset linkage rather than seller inactivity.
